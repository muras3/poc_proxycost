Proxycost Shape Book

Every item the comparison tool can price is drawn from one of archetypes. A family is a single drawing; its members differ only along a few axes: proportion, height, tone, thickness, how the face is divided (window, band, label, spine) and corner rounding. No new silhouette is invented per item, and the same item always gets the same shape — it is a fixed lookup, nothing is generated.

Not to scale

Shapes say what kind of thing an item is, not how large it is. We hold no dimension data; relative sizes inside a family are ordered, not measured.

Weights

Medians of listed weights in grams from the weight table, shown exactly as recorded with the p25–p75 band and sample count n. Nothing on this page is typed in by hand.

Long items

1-piece rods and kyudo bows may exceed carrier length limits. The limits are still being confirmed, so they are flagged here without a figure.

For the packing-box prototype. The vocabulary is a plain lookup: SHAPES[id] gives a family and its parameters, renderGlyph(id) returns the SVG. Ids are the weight-table line ids, so the box can call it directly on a matched line. Items whose subtype is unknown (reel, rod) are drawn as the family silhouette without any committing marks, with a dotted outline.

Source: lines69.json, 69 lines across 9 categories. n is the number of listings behind each median; music lines carry n = 0 as recorded.
