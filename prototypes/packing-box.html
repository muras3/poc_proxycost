proxycost — visual prototype

The box is the price

Japan Post EMS charges by weight in 42 discrete steps. Nothing else about the parcel moves the postage — not how full the box looks. This prototype makes the step you are standing on, and the distance to the next one, the thing you watch.

Shapes stand for what kind of thing it is, not its real size; every weight here is an estimate, so every item is drawn see-through. The box grows only when the weight step changes. The shading on the current step shows how far into that step the parcel is.

Every figure here came from running the real engine on this basket (5 items at ¥5,000 each, Yahoo! Auctions, shipped to the United States).

What this prototype refuses to do. It never shows the box filling up, because volume does not enter the EMS price at all — a box that looks full would teach you a cost model that is not real. It never invents a shape from a photo: each item is drawn from a fixed set of archetypes looked up from the weight table, so the same item always looks the same. Items whose weight is an estimate are drawn semi-transparent, so you can see which ones make the total uncertain just by looking in the box.
