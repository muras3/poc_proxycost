    How this is worked out

    What we add up

    For each service, one number: item price + service fee + domestic shipping + EMS international shipping + tax and customs fees in your country.

    What we don’t know

    • Weight. Listings don’t say. We guess from the category. It’s the biggest number in the total, so change it if you know better.
    • Whether Buyee, ZenMarket and FROM JAPAN charge exactly Japan Post EMS rates. Neokyo does. The others may add a margin; we couldn’t confirm.
    • Duty rates outside the US, above each country’s threshold. We leave duty out and say so.
    • Item origin. Many “Japanese” items are made in China. US duty depends on it.

    Who pays us

    • ZenMarket, Buyee and FROM JAPAN pay a referral fee if you sign up through our link. Neokyo pays nothing.
    • In our 60 test carts, on fees alone, Neokyo was cheapest in 59. The company we point to most often pays us nothing.

    Not covered

    • Mercari, Suruga-ya and other sites we can’t read from a server — type the price in.
    • Shipping other than EMS. Insurance. Items a carrier won’t take.
    • Countries other than the seven in the list.

    This is an estimate from published price lists, Japan Post EMS rates and the tax rules we could find, all checked on . It isn’t a quote. Companies change fees, carriers change rates, and customs decides what it decides. Not included: shipping methods other than EMS, insurance, your card’s currency fee, and items carriers refuse (batteries, liquids, aerosols).

    Links to ZenMarket, Buyee and FROM JAPAN are referral links: they pay us if you sign up or buy. Neokyo has no referral programme and pays us nothing. We rank by total cost and never by what a company pays us. Ads on this page are separate from the rankings.
