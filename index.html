What we add up

For each service, one number: item price + service fee + domestic shipping + EMS international shipping + tax and customs fees in your country.

What we don’t know

  • Weight. Listings don’t say. We guess from the category. It’s the biggest number in the total, so change it if you know better.
  • Whether Buyee, ZenMarket and FROM JAPAN charge exactly Japan Post EMS rates. Neokyo does. The others may add a margin; we couldn’t confirm.
  • Duty rates outside the US, above each country’s threshold. We leave duty out and say so.
  • Item origin. Many “Japanese” items are made in China. US duty depends on it. We apply the Japan rate.

Who pays us

  • ZenMarket, Buyee and FROM JAPAN pay a referral fee if you sign up through our link. Neokyo pays nothing.
  • Rankings use the total only; we never look at the referral amount.
  • In our 60 test carts, on fees alone, Neokyo was cheapest in 59. The company we point to most often pays us nothing.

Not covered

  • Mercari, Suruga-ya and other sites we can’t read from a server — you can type the price in.
  • Shipping other than EMS. Insurance. Items a carrier won’t take (batteries, aerosols, liquids).
  • Countries other than the seven in the list.
